Who We Are

The same eye we bring
to campaigns, turned
toward your day.

Anansé is a production house first. We spend our year directing brand films and shooting editorial, and we bring that discipline to weddings — lighting we have thought about, frames we have composed, a story with a shape rather than a folder of moments.

We take a small number of weddings a year on purpose. It is the only way the work stays this considered.

What We Offer

Three ways we work together

Every one of them can be photography, film, or both. We scope the day with you and quote from there — no packages to squeeze yourselves into.

01

Engagement

The shoot before the day. A relaxed session on location that gets you used to being photographed together — and gives you images for the save-the-date, the website, and the walls.

  • Location scouted with you
  • 1–3 hours, one or two looks
  • Stills, with a short film on request
02

Editorial Portraits

A directed portrait session with the styling, lighting and intent of a fashion editorial. For the couple, the wedding party, or the two of you after the day itself.

  • Creative direction and moodboard
  • Studio or location
  • Full retouching, editorial finish
03

Wedding Coverage

The day itself, from preparations through to the last dance. Mostly invisible, occasionally directive — we step in when a frame needs it and step back for everything else.

  • Half day through to multi-day
  • Second shooter where it earns its place
  • Private gallery and a film worth rewatching

Every wedding is scoped differently, so we quote rather than price off a list. Tell us the date, the place and roughly what you have in mind, and we will come back with a number.
